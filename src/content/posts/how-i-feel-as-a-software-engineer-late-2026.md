How do I feel today as a software engineer being swallowed by AI?

Some days I try to fight it. Some days I get excited about it, about how easily I can ship things in a day that used to take weeks using AI.

New models can do what I was proud of in a few seconds now. It was my "skills" for years: finding solutions, writing clean codes and knowing syntaxes. It kills all the motivation. I'm doing SWE because I love creating & building. If AI can do that now why should I resist!

From a single person's point of view this is frustrating but it makes sense when I see it through the lens of the progress of mankind. This is how we improved so far, this is how we solved all the problems through the history: by guessing and innovating stuff and fixing the errors. Every time some people were affected by it. Now it's my turn, the outcome of the new guesses is AI doing whatever I was doing even better & faster, and it should.

Seeing AI spreading this fast and things like my career slipping out of my control is exciting but scary at the same time. Needs changed so I need to change too, and I'm still figuring out how.

I first need to get myself motivated in a different way and get astonished by 1000s of unknown opportunities AI opened in front of me, like any other big innovation.

I now need to focus on what AI can't do. AI is doing a good job on pattern matching and predicting next move based on its context BUT it has no idea how to be a human.

So I'll continue to be a human and stop acting like an agent, let the idea of fighting AI go and try to be a person that can handle all aspects of what I was calling "product" or "building": product, marketing, sales, etc.
